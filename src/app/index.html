1 OPEN A SONG

2 PICK THE PARTS

Melody plays on the first motor. Fill plays in the melody's rests (a riff or lead guitar). Bass plays on the second motor. Each motor plays one note at a time: chords keep their top note (melody) or bottom note (bass).

3 SECTION AND SOUND

4 YOUR MACHINE

Steps/mm is the number already set in your firmware ($100 to $102 on grbl, M92 on Marlin). Microsteps is the driver setting: a stepper sings at its full-step rate, so this sets the pitch. Max rate and accel limit which notes your machine can reach.

5 YOUR G-CODE

LISTEN plays the two motor lines as tones at the pitch your machine will sing them. The program moves relative to wherever the tool is, stays inside the travel box, and ends where it started. Jog to a clear spot, keep hands off, and be ready to hit feed hold.
